Vanta

A rules-based Daily Sweep strategy research project designed around reproducible definitions and controlled testing.

← Back to research lab
The challenge

What problem needs solving?

Trading setups and discretionary chart patterns are notoriously difficult to compare, backtest, or automate without unambiguous signal definitions, consistent session time windows, and defensible execution risk controls. Without a formal requirements specification, strategy research easily falls into curve-fitting and survivorship bias.

My approach

How the work is structured

Applied Business Analysis rigor to quantitative strategy design: specifying exact daily bias rules, Asian/London/New York session time boundaries, liquidity sweep conditions, and multi-timeframe order entry criteria. Built automated historical data pipelines in Python interfacing with MetaTrader 5 (MT5), pairing automated pytest validation with strict risk-management models before any simulated paper execution.

Proof & next evidence

What to show the reviewer

Documenting clear specification sheets, data integrity checks, sample unit test outputs, and reproducible backtest logs. Note: All current visuals represent research and system architecture; no claims of guaranteed returns, live profitability, or win rates are asserted without third-party audited execution records.

Next iteration

What comes next?

Finalizing comprehensive Monte Carlo scenario stress-testing, out-of-sample data validation across historical XAUUSD market regimes, slippage/spread variance modeling, and establishing independent verification criteria prior to paper forward testing.
