The challenge

What problem needs solving?

Rapidly built AI tools and freelance web applications frequently ship with invisible edge-case failures: broken responsive breakpoints, unhandled network timeouts, silent runtime errors, and confusing conversion funnels that damage client trust. Engineering teams often struggle to translate deep technical bugs into clear business risks for non-technical stakeholders.

My approach

Automated inspection & structured taxonomy

Engineered an end-to-end Python inspection framework coupled with a standardized 50+ metric checklist grounded in Nebiant Certified Business Analyst (NCBA™) criteria:

  • Audit Engine: Python evaluation script parsing structured inspection checklists, HTTP status codes, and console error monitors.
  • Severity Categorization: Standardized taxonomy sorting findings into Critical, Major, Minor, and Polish tiers with associated business risk ratings.
  • Automated Artifacts: Compiles structured Markdown reports and executive summary scorecards suitable for technical leads and product stakeholders.
  • Production Audits: Applied directly to audit, harden, and verify FLIPhub.ng and Bamba Shop prior to public deployment.
Business analysis highlights

Bridging engineering with executive value

Applied formal business analysis to quality assurance:

  • Built directly on Nebiant Certified Business Analyst (NCBA™) frameworks for risk assessment and requirements traceability.
  • Bridges technical code inspection with business value, customer conversion rates, and downtime risk mitigation.
  • Enforces repeatable verification checklists to eliminate regression defects across release cycles.
Proof & next evidence

What to show the reviewer

In accordance with strict truth-in-advertising standards, no synthetic or unverified screenshots are presented. The sidebar dossier features the CSS/text evaluation matrix representing the structured taxonomy and severity criteria utilized across audit engagements.
